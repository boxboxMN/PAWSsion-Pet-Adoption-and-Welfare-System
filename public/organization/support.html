Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from organizations.

Go to the Pets module to add new pets and manage existing profiles. You can update basic information, descriptions, images, health and vaccination status, personality tags, adoption status, and medical history.
Use the Donations module to manage your organization's donation information and review donation records. Make sure your payment details and donation instructions are kept accurate and up to date.
Pawpon compares an adopter's preferences with available pet information, including behavior, personality, age, and sex. The system then generates matching scores to help identify pets that may be suitable for the adopter.
Go to your organization's Profile section to update available information such as your organization name, description, contact details, and profile information. Keeping these details accurate helps adopters and supporters identify and contact your organization.
When contacting Pawpon Support, describe the problem clearly and mention which module is affected. Include what you were trying to do and what happened instead. Providing specific details helps the support team resolve the issue faster.
Make sure the pet's profile is complete and its Adoption Status is set appropriately. Also check that the pet has the necessary information and image required for display. Keeping the pet's profile updated helps ensure accurate information is shown to adopters.
First, check that the information you entered is complete and try the action again. If the issue continues, contact Pawpon Support and provide the affected module, the action you were performing, and a description of what happened so the support team can investigate and resolve the issue quickly.
